Weapons

SnarlyHydra published on 2026-01-01

This was fairly simple to create, as the main blade only required a plane. I used a reference image of a sword and traced the outline with a plane. I then extruded it and added faces, allowing for a more solid look. The hilt required a cube to be scaled and extruded to fit to size. I struggled a little bit with trying to extrude the faces of the blade’s plane out, as some of the faces weren’t selecting. But I just re-did the blade, which solved that.

Clay Girl

SnarlyHydra published on 2025-12-19

This was my first time creating a human character in Blender, and my first time designing hair. This character was fairly simple, with extruding objects and moving vertices being the main focus. I got to explore the mirror modifier, and understand how that works in relation to where an objects point of origin is placed. I would like to attempt to rig this character, so I can give it a walk cycle. At a later date, I would also like to try and add more detail to the face to actually give it some features.
